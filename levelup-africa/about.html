About LEVEL-UP AFRICA

LEVEL-UP AFRICA is a youth-driven platform committed to empowering African students and young professionals with practical skills, innovative thinking, and real-world exposure needed to thrive in today’s digital economy.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between classroom education and industry demands by creating opportunities for learning, networking, mentorship, and entrepreneurship.

FLAGSHIP PROJECT 2026

The LEVEL-UP AFRICA TECH & BUSINESS CONFERENCE 2026 EDITION (LATBC) is our major annual event designed to bring together students, entrepreneurs, industry leaders, and innovators.

LATBC 2026 will provide insights into technology, business, leadership, innovation, and personal development while creating a platform for collaboration and growth.

Event Details

Venue: Julius Onah Hall, ESUT, Enugu
