About the Conference

The Level-Up Africa Tech & Business Conference is a one-day annual gathering designed to equip students with insights, skills, and opportunities in technology and business.

Objectives

  • Expose students to real-world career paths
  • Inspire innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Connect students with industry professionals
  • Build a community of future leaders

Who Should Attend

  • University students
  • Recent graduates
  • Aspiring tech professionals
  • Entrepreneurs
  • Creatives

What You Will Gain

  • Practical career knowledge
  • Networking opportunities
  • Mentorship access
  • Industry exposure

Sample Agenda

  • 12:00 PM - Opening & Welcome
  • 12:20 PM - Keynote Session
  • 1:00 PM - Tech Session
  • 2:00 PM - Business & Strategy Session
  • 3:00 PM - Skills & Monetization
  • 4:00 PM - Panel & Q&A
  • 5:00 PM - Networking & Closing
